Cash offer questions

What sellers ask us before they send an address.

Do I need to make repairs before selling?

No. We buy in any condition. Leave the roof, the plumbing and the clean-out to us.

Do I pay agent commissions or fees?

No commissions, no listing costs and no hidden fees taken out of your proceeds.

How fast can you close?

Closing can happen in as little as 7 days, or later if you need more time. You pick the date.

What kinds of property do you buy?

Single-family homes, multifamily property, and vacant land or buildable lots. Condos are reviewed case by case. We also buy luxury homes and commercial property; every property is reviewed on its own numbers.

Which areas do you buy in?

Baltimore and Maryland first, with buying markets in Florida and North Carolina. The ZIP codes we work in are listed on the areas we buy page.

Is the cash offer really no obligation?

Yes. Requesting an offer is free and creates no contract. Any price we discuss is an indication only until it is put in writing and signed by both parties.

Will you call or text me?

Only as you consent. The form has two optional text-message consent boxes, both unticked by default, and consent is not a condition of purchase. You can reply STOP to stop text messages or HELP for help, and you can ask us to stop contacting you at any time.

How do you work out the offer?

We review the property and comparable sales nearby, then present a fair cash offer.

What if I change my mind?

Nothing is binding until you sign. You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time by emailing info@intensepropertysolutions.com.

Still have a question?

Call or text +1 443-876-5673, or send the address and we will answer it with the property in front of us.
