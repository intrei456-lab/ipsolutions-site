A real-estate investment company buying houses directly from owners, with no agent in the middle.

What we do

Intense Property Solutions LLC buys houses for cash. We work with owners who want to sell without repairs, showings or agent commissions, and we buy in any condition.

Our primary market is Baltimore and Maryland. We also buy in Florida and North Carolina, and the ZIP codes we work in are listed on the areas we buy page.

How we work

You send the address. We review the property and comparable sales nearby and come back with a cash offer. If you accept, we handle the paperwork and close on the date you choose. No commissions, no listing costs and no hidden fees taken out of your proceeds.

Any price we discuss is an indication only until it is put in writing and signed by both parties. You are never obligated by asking for an offer.
